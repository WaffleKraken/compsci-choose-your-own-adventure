## How the chapters connect

```
Crippling Debt (IRS calls, owe $6,700)
   |
   +-- Ignore it ----------------------------------> END 1  Left on Read
   |
   +-- Get a job --> J*b (order memory game, $1 per correct order)
   |                    |
   |                    +-- Give up ----------------> END 2  Lifer
   |                    +-- Quit / fired (3 strikes) --+
   |                                                   |
   +-- Go to casino -----------------------------------+
                                                       v
                          On the Way to the Casino (+$65, 3 random bosses)
                          Mugger · Beggar · Samaritan · Hunger · Snake
                             |  hit $0 -------------> END 3  Stranded
                             |  can't pay Popeyes --> END 4  Dine and Dash
                             |  ride, 2% -----------> END 5  Kidnapped
                             |  no ointment, 30% ---> END 6  Snakebite
                             |  Beggar, 1% ---------> END 7  Miracle
                             v
                  Casino Pt. 1 (doorman tour, 20 rounds until sunrise)
                             |  hit $0 -------------> END 8  The House Always Wins
                             |  out of rounds ------> END 9  Sunrise
                             v  reach $6,700
                  Cashing Out (Casino Pt. 2)
                                pay the IRS --------> END 10 Debt Free
                                high roller, win ---> END 11 High Roller
                                high roller, lose --> END 12 Greed
                                Claude tokens ------> END 13 Relapse
```

Each chapter page ends with an **Open questions** section listing what's still undecided for that part of the story.
